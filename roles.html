Class Basics
How do classes work?
Starting out

Starting Characters automatically begin their Role Ability at Rank 4 (unless the GM of your particular game decides to allow you to start at a higher Rank).

In the 2020s, Roles were basically the "job" your Character had. Roles defined what they did, but not what they were. But in the Time of the Red, the lack of structure and steady "jobs" means that a Role is more of a self-defining label.

You don't "do Solo stuff." You are a Solo—it's what defines you no matter what you do. No one in the Time of the Red pays you to punch a clock as a Solo— instead you look for gigs that are the kind of gigs a Solo would do.

In short, in the Time of the Red you are your own boss and your Role is your personal job description.

But since you are new to The Street, you're not starting at the top. Nope, you're starting near the bottom.

Rockerboy
Rock-and-roll rebels who use performance, art, and rhetoric to fight authority
Role ability: Charismatic Impact

The Rockerboy has the Role ability Charismatic Impact. They can influence others by sheer presence of personality. They need not be a musical performer; they can influence others through poetry, art, dance, or simply their physical presence. They could be a rocker—or a cult leader.

Influence [Rank + 1d10]

The Rockerboy can convince a group of fans to preform actions dependant on the rockerboys rank. the dv to succeed is dependant on the group size

If the group of people are not already fans of the rockerboy, the rockerboy is not in combat, and the group does not dislike them they can make an influence roll to convert the group into fans

A single person [dv8]

A small group (up to 6) [dv10]

A large group [dv12]

Charismatic Impact Ranks 1 and 2

Venues You Can Play: Small local clubs

Impact on a Single Fan (DV8): Rockerboy can convince a fan to do a small favor; buy them a drink or meal, give them a lift, etc.

Impact on a Small Group of Fans (DV10): Fans ask for autographs and personal totems; stop Rockerboy in the streets to befriend them.

Impact on a Huge Group of Fans (DV12): You're kidding, right? You don't have huge groups of fans yet.

Charismatic Impact Ranks 3 and 4

Venues You Can Play: Well-known clubs

Impact on a Single Fan (DV8): Rockerboy can convince a fan to do a major favor; go to bed with them, put in a good word, etc.

Impact on a Small Group of Fans (DV10): Convince a group of fans to regularly hang out and provide party favors like booze or drugs.

Impact on a Huge Group of Fans (DV12): Rockerboy has a strong local following that buys their recordings and merch.

Charismatic Impact Ranks 5 and 6

Venues You Can Play: Large, important clubs

Impact on a Single Fan (DV8): Rockerboy can convince a fan to commit a minor crime; shoplift, help in a fight, etc.

Impact on a Small Group of Fans (DV10): Convince fans to be the Rockerboy’s personal "posse"; constantly hang out, do favors, and provide for them.

Impact on a Huge Group of Fans (DV12): Fans are spread throughout the city (and nearby cities), loyal and willing to do major favors for attention.

Charismatic Impact Ranks 7 and 8

Venues You Can Play: Small concert halls, local video feeds

Impact on a Single Fan (DV8): Fan is willing to risk their life for the Rockerboy without question.

Impact on a Small Group of Fans (DV10): Convince fans to commit minor crimes; shoplift, help in a fight, etc.

Impact on a Huge Group of Fans (DV12): Fans are rabidly loyal; they fight rivals, maintain strong information networks, and band together to help.

Charismatic Impact Rank 9

Venues You Can Play: Large concert halls, national video feed

Impact on a Single Fan (DV8): Convince a fan to commit a major crime; steal expensive items, beat someone up.

Impact on a Small Group of Fans (DV10): Convince fans to commit major crimes; steal or assault on behalf of the Rockerboy.

Impact on a Huge Group of Fans (DV12): Fans are essentially brainwashed; they will riot, destroy property, and even kill for the Rockerboy.

Charismatic Impact Rank 10

Venues You Can Play: Huge stadiums or international video feeds

Impact on a Single Fan (DV8): Fan is willing to sacrifice themselves for the Rockerboy without question.

Impact on a Small Group of Fans (DV10): Convince fans to risk their lives as personal protection for the Rockerboy.

Impact on a Huge Group of Fans (DV12): Fans are a global, cult-like following; they will act as a private army, doing almost anything for the Rockerboy if asked.
